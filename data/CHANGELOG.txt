Changes to the spektrafilm profiles and LUTs shipped in this folder
====================================================================

The spektrafilm profiles, filter data and LUTs in this folder are by Andrea
Volpato (https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) and are licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0; see SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt. This file records how Negs' copies
differ from upstream, as that license asks.

2026-10: updated to the upstream experimental branch, commit 28bf883

- profiles/: the colour and B&W profiles are verbatim copies of 28bf883's
  src/spektrafilm/data/profiles. Unchanged.
- filters/: the dichroic, heat-absorbing and lens-transmission data are
  verbatim copies of 28bf883's. Unchanged. neutral_print_filters.json is
  28bf883's with Negs-only entries added (below); upstream's entries are
  unchanged.
- luts/spectral_upsampling/: hanatos2025_irradiance_xy_tc.{npy,toml},
  arctic2026beta04_reflectance_xy_tc.{npy,toml},
  mallett2019_reflectance_xy_tc.{npy,toml} and
  hanatos_irradiance_xy_coeffs_250304.lut are verbatim copies of 28bf883's
  src/spektrafilm/data/luts/spectral_upsampling. Unchanged. Earlier Negs
  versions shipped the hanatos2025 table as irradiance_xy_tc.npy (same bytes)
  and the arctic2026alpha02 tables, which upstream has since removed.

Negs-only data, not from spektrafilm:

- profiles/kodak_trix400_d76_8min.json and
  profiles/ilford_mgrc_deluxe_grade2.json are new preparations from Kodak and
  Ilford datasheets, in spektrafilm's profile format. Sources and method:
  profile-sources/bw-expansion/README.md.
- filters/neutral_print_filters.json: entries for kodak_trix400_d76_8min on
  every colour paper under TH-KG3, derived with upstream's own solver
  (runtime/print_balance.solve_neutral_filter_shifts at 28bf883) by
  scripts/derive_neutral_print_filters.py, which first re-derives upstream's
  entries for Double-X and three colour negatives to check the method. B&W
  papers (kodak_2302, ilford_mgrc_deluxe_grade2) have no colour to balance,
  so they get no entries and keep the C0 M50 Y50 fallback.

2026-11: four more Negs-only preparations from manufacturer datasheets

- profiles/agfa_apx25.json, profiles/agfa_apx100.json and
  profiles/agfa_apx400.json are new single-channel B&W preparations from the
  Agfa APX datasheets (status 08/1995), printing to
  ilford_mgrc_deluxe_grade2. Sources, derived axis calibration and
  assumptions: profile-sources/agfa-apx25, agfa-apx100 and agfa-apx400.
  Development conditions differ in how well they resolve and each recipe says
  so: APX 25's gradient matches every listed developer at its recommended
  time, APX 100's is about 0.8 of recommended, and APX 400's falls below
  everything on its own gamma-time panel, so its 6 minutes is a nominal label.
- profiles/kodak_ektachrome64_epr.json is a new colour preparation from Kodak
  publication E-8, the first needing no donor dye curves because that sheet
  plots C, M and Y separately. Sources and method:
  profile-sources/kodak-ektachrome64-epr. Two things in it are not the
  stock's own and the profile's datasource says so: its spectral adaptation
  parameters are borrowed from kodak_ektachrome_100, and its density curves
  are balanced against a rendered grey ramp rather than left as the sheet
  plots them.
- profile-sources/ektachrome100-control holds upstream's own digitised
  Ektachrome 100 points, reused as a fixture so this pipeline can be measured
  against a profile whose finished form already ships. No profile is built
  from it for the catalogue.
- filters/neutral_print_filters.json is unchanged: the three APX films print
  to a B&W paper, which has no colour to balance, and the EPR preparation is
  a reversal film with no print stage.

  Axis calibration for all four is derived from the page by
  scripts/bw_expansion/extract_graphs.py rather than measured by hand, which
  is the one methodological difference from the 2026-10 B&W preparations.

- profiles/kodak_kodachrome_200.json is a further colour preparation, from
  Kodak publication E-55 (June 2009) page 5. Its density curves are balanced
  against a rendered grey ramp at data_trustability 0.2 rather than upstream's
  0.5: at 0.5 the stock sits 11.8% off neutral across five stops, which 0.2
  takes to 3.7% with density corrections under 12%. Spectral adaptation
  parameters are borrowed from kodak_kodachrome_64, the same K-14 family.
- profile-sources/kodachrome64-control rebuilds shipped kodak_kodachrome_64
  from the same sheet, extraction included, as an end-to-end check: it renders
  at mean chroma 67.6 against the shipped profile's 65.9. No profile from it
  is shipped.
- profiles/kodak_ektachrome_64t_epy.json is a colour preparation from Kodak
  publication E-130, and the first tungsten-balanced still film here: the
  sheet states tungsten exposure, so the profile declares reference_illuminant
  "T" and the preparation balances its channels against a grey under that
  illuminant rather than D55. It fits its source better than any other colour
  stock prepared here (0.0040 against a 0.02 gate) and reaches 1.2% off
  neutral across five stops at upstream's default balance bias. Its spectral
  adaptation parameters are borrowed from kodak_ektachrome_100, which is a
  daylight stock - a known imperfection recorded in its recipe.
- profiles/kodak_ektachrome_400x_epl.json and profiles/kodak_ektachrome_e200.json
  are two further colour preparations, from Kodak publications E-161 and E-28,
  both reversal films with the full panel set and no donor dye curves. Each is
  balanced at upstream's default bias, reaching 3.5% and 1.3% off neutral across
  five stops. Adaptation parameters are borrowed from kodak_ektachrome_100.
  A third sheet from the same survey, Ektachrome P1600 (E-147), was prepared and
  held back: it fails its fit tolerance at 0.0353 for reasons not yet diagnosed.

2026-11: naming convention for source-based preparations

- Every profile prepared here from a datasheet, rather than copied from
  spektrafilm, now carries a leading asterisk on its catalogue name instead of
  a trailing "(experimental)". Ten profiles are affected, including the two
  from the 2026-10 B&W expansion, so the picker does not mix two conventions.
  Asterisks sort ahead of letters, which groups them at the top of the film
  list. "Professional" is also dropped from the Kodak slide names, which were
  running long; the four upstream Endura paper profiles keep theirs, since
  they are verbatim copies and were not part of this change.
- profiles/kodak_ektachrome_160t_ept.json is a colour preparation from Kodak
  publication E-144, and the first whose panels are not all on one page: its
  characteristic curves are on page 4 and the other two panels on page 5, so
  each graph in the recipe names its own page. Tungsten-balanced like 64T.
  Fit residual 0.0109 against the 0.02 gate, 2.9% off neutral at upstream's
  default balance bias.
- profiles/fujifilm_provia_400x.json and profiles/fujifilm_t64.json are the
  first Fujifilm preparations here, from Fujifilm AF3-0213E and AF3-178E.
  Both plot their own C, M and Y dye densities, but no visual neutral, so the
  dyes' relative strengths are set only by the densitometer normalisation -
  one constraint fewer than the Kodak sheets. T64 is tungsten-type. Fit
  residuals 0.0049 and 0.0145; 3.3% and 1.3% off neutral. Adaptation
  parameters borrowed from fujifilm_provia_100f.

2026-11: the first colour negatives, and their print filters

- profiles/kodak_portra_160nc.json, kodak_portra_160vc.json and
  kodak_portra_400vc.json are the first colour negatives prepared here, from
  Kodak publication E-190 (2006), one stock per page. A negative's sheet gives
  two things the reversal sheets do not: D-min, which is the orange mask and
  becomes a measured spectral base density, and a midscale neutral, which puts
  the mid-grey anchor on a measurement rather than a convention. Dye shapes
  come from kodak_portra_400, scaled so their mixture reproduces each sheet's
  own midscale-neutral-minus-D-min. Portra 400NC is not shipped: its channel
  fit does not converge.
- filters/neutral_print_filters.json gains entries for those three films on
  every colour paper, derived with scripts/derive_neutral_print_filters.py,
  which first re-derives upstream's own entries and agrees with them to 0.124
  filter units. Without them the print fell back to C0 M50 Y50 and a grey ramp
  arrived 29-41% off neutral; with them it starts at 9-10%, and after
  balancing the three sit at 5.6% to 9.1%. For scale, the shipped references
  score 13.0% (Portra 400), 15.9% (Portra 160) and 10.4% (Gold 200) on the
  same ramp, so these are slightly better than all three. A negative is
  balanced through the print, as upstream's corrector does.
- Every colour profile here now writes base_density flat, one value per
  wavelength, which is what the shipped colour profiles carry and what
  upstream's printing path expects; the nested form is for a B&W
  development-time family and broadcasts wrongly. The eight reversal profiles
  are regenerated accordingly, with unchanged numbers.
- profiles/kodak_bright_sun_ga.json is a further colour negative, from Kodak
  publication E-2328 (July 2003), whose dye panel sits on its own page. Dye
  shapes come from kodak_gold_200, a consumer negative like this one, scaled
  against the sheet's own midscale-neutral-minus-D-min. Fit residual 0.0064.
  Its print-filter entries are derived alongside the Portra variants, taking
  the database to 40 Negs-only entries; 20.2% off neutral before balancing and
  5.3% after, the best of the negatives prepared here.
